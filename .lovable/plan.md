- Align descriptions beneath their titles, using brighter larger titles and quieter readable descriptions.
- Add consistent vertical spacing across all six items while keeping the right column balanced with the headline and illustration.
- Mark the selected item with the existing mint accent on its icon and title plus a short thin vertical marker.
- Keep hover, click, keyboard focus, automatic selection, and illustration switching unchanged.
- Retain the Denied permissions block beneath the list with more separation and its existing thin green line and copy.
- Preserve the existing stacked mobile layout and prevent title or description clipping.
