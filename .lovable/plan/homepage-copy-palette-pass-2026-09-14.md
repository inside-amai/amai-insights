# Homepage Copy Palette Pass

## Goal
Refine `/homepagecopy` into a more cohesive, sophisticated expression of the supplied aqua, violet, black, gray, and white palette without changing page content or structure.

## Visual approach
- Keep black and graphite as the dominant canvas, with white and gray typography preserving the current hierarchy.
- Use aqua as the primary functional accent for evidence, active states, trust signals, and fine architectural details.
- Introduce violet selectively as a secondary atmospheric accent, mainly in quiet light fields, key transitions, and paired aqua/violet moments.
- Replace out-of-palette accent colors and the bone-colored section with treatments derived from the approved palette.
- Preserve the restrained institutional tone: no loud gradients, decorative blobs, or excessive glow.

## Implementation
- Add semantic aqua and violet surface, line, glow, and text tokens to the global design system.
- Apply a page-scoped palette treatment to the hero, pool table, TARI feature, evidence section, FAQ, process sections, light operator section, Lens section, outro, and overlays.
- Update supporting visuals used on this page, including the gauge and evidence chart, to use the same palette while retaining their data, interactions, and animation behavior.
- Keep all existing copy, ordering, links, functionality, spacing, and responsive composition intact.

## Validation
- Check the page at desktop and mobile widths.
- Confirm text contrast, interaction states, charts, lightboxes, and section transitions remain clear.
- Confirm the project build and browser runtime remain clean.
